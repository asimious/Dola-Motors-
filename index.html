About Dola Motors

More Than a Rental. Your Journey Partner.

Dola Motors began in Kasur with a handful of well-kept cars and a simple promise: every customer drives away in a vehicle we'd trust with our own family.

Today our fleet spans city hatchbacks to executive saloons, 12-seat vans and weekend sports cars — each inspected, cleaned and insured before every trip, and backed by a team that answers the phone at 3 AM.

We know the roads between Kasur, Lahore and beyond, and we price the way we'd want to be priced: clearly, fairly, with nothing hidden at return.

Frequently asked

What do I need to rent a car?

A valid CNIC or passport, a valid driving licence and a refundable security deposit.

Do you deliver the car?

Yes. Delivery anywhere within Kasur is free; Lahore city and airport delivery are available for a fee.

Can I cancel my booking?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pick-up. Later cancellations are charged one rental day.

How do I pay?

Cash on pickup, bank transfer, card, JazzCash or Easypaisa.
